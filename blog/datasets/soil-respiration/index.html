Global Soil Respiration: A Climate-Driven Model from a Global Database

A paper-based source for modeled soil respiration rates and their global spatiotemporal interpretation.

Jul 20263 min readDataset note

The data and the model are inseparable here.

The source cited in the screenshot is a global soil-respiration database used to build a climate-driven model. It should be documented as a paper-derived input, with the model version and variable definition attached.

Paper and database

The cited work is Hashimoto et al., Global spatiotemporal distribution of soil respiration modeled using a global database, published in Biogeosciences. The study uses a global soil respiration dataset to model spatial and temporal variation in soil respiration from climate drivers.

For a data inventory, this entry belongs under “paper-derived environmental data,” not under a generic portal category. The paper explains how observations were assembled, modeled, and interpreted.

Use in modeling

Soil respiration can act as an ecological carbon-cycle covariate when a study needs terrestrial background conditions. Before merging it with river or vessel variables, preserve the temporal period, units, spatial resolution, and whether the value is observed, modeled, or aggregated.

The safest citation is the article DOI plus the exact table, supplement, or derived file used in your workflow.
