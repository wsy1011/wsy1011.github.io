Downscaled Gridded Global GDP: From Country Totals to Spatial Exposure

A Zenodo dataset for spatially explicit GDP per capita at purchasing power parity over 1990–2022.

Jul 20264 min readDataset note

Spatial GDP is an allocation, not a local account.

This dataset makes national or regional economic values usable in grid-based analysis. The grid is valuable precisely because it provides spatial context, but its cells should not be interpreted as independently observed local GDP accounts.

What the Zenodo record provides

The official Zenodo record describes a downscaled global dataset of GDP per capita at PPP for 1990–2022. It is designed to support spatial analyses where country totals alone are too coarse.

The record is the right citation and provenance anchor for a pipeline. It also makes the version and access date explicit, which matters because spatial socioeconomic datasets are often updated or mirrored elsewhere.

How I would use it

Use the raster as a contextual covariate for exposure, accessibility, regional comparisons, or economic weighting. Before joining it to river or port data, check the grid resolution, coordinate reference system, units, missing-value convention, and whether the analysis needs GDP per capita or an aggregate GDP measure.

The key modeling decision is not “which cell is richest?” but “what spatial question can this allocation support without overstating precision?”
