WorldClim v2.1: Global Climate Surfaces at Research-Friendly Scales

A practical climate baseline for spatial modeling, with monthly temperature, precipitation, and bioclimatic variables.

Jul 20264 min readDataset note

WorldClim is a baseline, not a weather station.

WorldClim v2.1 provides interpolated global climate surfaces for 1970–2000, with resolutions from 30 seconds to 10 minutes. It is designed for mapping and spatial modeling.

What is available

The official WorldClim v2.1 page provides monthly minimum, mean, and maximum temperature, precipitation, solar radiation, wind speed, water vapor pressure, and 19 bioclimatic variables. The historical layers are distributed as GeoTIFF files.

The bioclimatic variables compress monthly climate into interpretable summaries such as annual precipitation, temperature seasonality, and wettest-quarter conditions. That makes them convenient for global regressions and ecological or environmental covariates.

How I would use it

For a river or shipping study, WorldClim can provide a consistent climate background for temperature and precipitation. I would keep the version, period, resolution, units, and resampling method with the extracted raster values.

Do not mix WorldClim's 1970–2000 climatology with a claim about a specific year's weather without an explicit downscaling or reanalysis step.
