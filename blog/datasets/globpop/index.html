GlobPOP: A Global Gridded Population Dataset for Spatial Context

A 33-year, 1990–2022 global gridded population dataset distributed through the Google Earth Engine community catalogue.

Jul 20264 min readDataset note

Population grids are useful because they preserve geography.

GlobPOP helps translate population from administrative summaries into a spatial layer that can be joined with rivers, roads, ports, and environmental conditions.

What it is

The GlobPOP project page describes a 33-year global gridded population dataset generated with cluster analysis and statistical learning. The associated Zenodo record provides the dataset provenance and citation details.

The Earth Engine catalogue is convenient for exploratory mapping and reproducible raster workflows. It also reduces the friction of moving from a global population question to a spatial mask or regional summary.

Good uses and limits

GlobPOP is a sensible input for population exposure, demand proxies, accessibility analysis, and population-weighted environmental summaries. Keep its year and spatial resolution explicit, and do not confuse a modeled grid with a census observation at every pixel.

When combining population with GDP, climate, or river data, align years and aggregation rules first. The join is often more consequential than the choice of visualization.
