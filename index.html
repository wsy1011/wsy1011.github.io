Research Focus

I am Suyang Wang, a Ph.D. student at Hohai University. My research is dedicated to sustainable waterway transportation and the decarbonization of inland shipping.

Methodologically, I specialize in integrating hydrodynamic numerical simulation with operations optimization. By leveraging this interdisciplinary approach, I explore sustainable logistics solutions to drive emission reductions and enhance the efficiency of inland waterway networks.
