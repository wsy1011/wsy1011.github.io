Research Focus

I am Suyang Wang, a Ph.D. student at Hohai University. My research focuses on sustainable waterway transportation and shipping decarbonization.

Methodologically, I specialize in integrating hydrodynamic numerical simulation with operations optimization. By leveraging this interdisciplinary approach, I explore sustainable logistics solutions to reduce emissions and improve the efficiency and resilience of waterway, port, and shipping networks.
